# Past-paper analysis

1. **List the papers** with codes:
   - P = prof/final
   - S = sendup / pre-final
   - T1 / T2 = 1st / 2nd term
   - plus the year (e.g. S25, T1-26)

   Note any missing papers or pages.
2. **Make one row per topic**, merging wording variants ("cirrhosis causes" and "cirrhosis with pigment" are the same topic). Columns:
   - Topic
   - S/P count (how many sendups and profs asked it)
   - Old-term count
   - Years
   - **Angle asked** (exactly what they asked: "causes, special stains, labs")
   - This year's term? (⚑)
3. **Rank** by S/P count, then old-term count. Ties go to the more recent topic.
4. **List the asked-once topics** separately. They still get their 1–2 most testable cards.
5. **Copy this year's term questions verbatim** at the end; they become `TermAsked` cards.
6. **Look for patterns.** Usually:
   - term papers repeat almost word for word year to year
   - sendups reuse many of that year's term topics

   State these patterns, because they decide what to revise last.
7. **Every priority row needs a card for each angle asked.** Track it with ☐ / ☑.
